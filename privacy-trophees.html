Trophees is a personal collection tracker for iOS. Your privacy matters. This policy explains what data the app uses and how.

1) Data We Collect

Trophees does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. Everything stays on your device.

2) Photos & Camera

Trophees can access your camera and photo library so you can add images to your collection items. Photos are stored locally on your device only and are never uploaded or shared.

3) App Settings

Your preferences (such as accent color and display settings) are saved locally using iOS standard storage (UserDefaults). This data never leaves your device.

4) Third-Party Services

4.1 Trophees does not use any analytics, advertising, or third-party tracking services.

Your data will be passed on in accordance with Article 6 Paragraph 1 Letter b GDPR exclusively for the purpose of payment processing and only to the extent that it is necessary for this. Further information on data protection by RevenueCat can be found here: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

5) Data Retention

All data is stored locally on your device. Deleting the app removes all associated data permanently.

6) Children's Privacy

Trophees does not knowingly collect any information from children under the age of 13.
