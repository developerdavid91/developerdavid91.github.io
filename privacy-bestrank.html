Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 4, 2026  ·  App: BestRank

Responsible for data processing regarding this app within the meaning of the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) is David Schmidt, Harthauser Straße 20, 67374 Hanhofen, Germany, email: david91schmidt@gmail.com

BestRank is an iOS app for creating tier lists and rankings. Your privacy matters. This policy explains what data the app uses, when data leaves your device, and what rights you have.

1) Data on Your Device

Your lists, tiers, items and images are stored locally on your device only. BestRank has no user accounts and no server of its own, and we have no access to your content. The app contains no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.

Data only leaves your device in the cases described in sections 4 to 6, or when you share an exported image yourself using the iOS share sheet.

2) Photos & Camera

BestRank only accesses the camera or your photos when you start an action that requires it:

  • Camera: to take a photo for an item. iOS asks for your permission the first time.
  • Choosing photos: the system photo picker is used. The app only receives the photos you select and has no access to the rest of your library.
  • Saving to Photos: to save an exported image of a list to your photo library. iOS asks for your permission the first time.

Photos you add are stored locally in the app and are never uploaded by BestRank.

3) App Settings

Your preferences (such as appearance and language) are saved locally using iOS standard storage (UserDefaults). This data never leaves your device.

4) Pasting and Loading Images from a Link

BestRank only reads the clipboard when you tap the Paste button.

If you paste a web address to add an image, BestRank downloads the image directly from that address. As with any web request, the server at that address receives your IP address and technical request data (for example the time of the request and a technical identifier of the app). That server is operated by a third party chosen by you; we have no influence on its data processing. BestRank does not keep cookies or other stored data between these requests. The downloaded image is stored locally on your device.

Legal basis: Art. 6 (1) (b) GDPR, since the download is carried out at your request.

5) Purchases (Apple and RevenueCat)

BestRank can be used for free. "BestRank Unlimited" is an optional one-time in-app purchase, not a subscription.

5.1 Apple

Purchases are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. Payment details, your name and your Apple Account are handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy and are never shared with us. Further information: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

5.2 RevenueCat

To verify purchases and unlock BestRank Unlimited, the app uses the service RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc., USA). When the app starts and when you buy or restore a purchase, the following data is transmitted to RevenueCat:

  • a random, anonymous user ID generated by the app (not linked to your name, email address or Apple Account),
  • your purchase status and the purchase receipt provided by Apple,
  • technical data such as IP address, device model, iOS version, app version and language/region settings.

This data is used exclusively to verify your purchase and provide the features you bought. Legal basis: Art. 6 (1) (b) GDPR.

RevenueCat is based in the United States. Data is transferred to the USA on the basis of the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework, insofar as the recipient is certified under it, and otherwise on the basis of the EU Standard Contractual Clauses (Art. 46 (2) (c) GDPR).

Further information on data protection by RevenueCat can be found here: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

6) Feedback by Email

If you choose to send feedback from the app's settings, an email is prepared in your mail app. Besides your message and your email address, it contains the app version, the iOS version and the device model identifier (for example "iPhone17,1"). You can review or remove this information before sending. Nothing is sent unless you send the email yourself.

We use this data only to answer your request and delete it once it has been handled, unless statutory retention obligations apply. Legal basis: Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR; our legitimate interest is responding to your inquiry.

7) Data Retention

Content and settings stored in the app remain on your device until you delete them. Deleting the app removes all associated data permanently. Data processed by RevenueCat is subject to RevenueCat's retention rules; feedback emails are handled as described in section 6.

8) Your Rights

Under the GDPR, you have the right to access your personal data (Art. 15), to rectification (Art. 16), to erasure (Art. 17), to restriction of processing (Art. 18), to data portability (Art. 20) and to object to processing based on legitimate interests (Art. 21). To exercise these rights, please contact us at the email address below. Since we do not operate user accounts, we may need information from you (such as the content of an email you sent) to identify your data.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority. The authority responsible for us is the State Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information of Rhineland-Palatinate (Der Landesbeauftragte für den Datenschutz und die Informationsfreiheit Rheinland-Pfalz): https://www.datenschutz.rlp.de

9) Children's Privacy

BestRank does not knowingly collect any information from children under the age of 13.

10) Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
